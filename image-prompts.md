# Sparr Foundation — Image prompts

Photography that protects the mission: **dignity-first, hopeful, documentary** — never
pity or poverty-porn. Show people with agency, warmth and a future.

## Shared style line (paste at the end of EVERY prompt)
> Documentary editorial photography, natural warm light, candid and authentic,
> shallow depth of field, soft amber and terracotta colour grade to match brand,
> gentle film grain, dignified and hopeful mood, real diverse people, photorealistic,
> 35mm. No text, no logos, no watermark, no staged stock-photo feel.

A consistent warm/amber grade across all images makes the site feel designed, not pasted.
